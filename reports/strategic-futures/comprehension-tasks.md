# Three prototype comprehension tasks

Proposed tasks for voluntary readers without prompt history. These are not a
representative usability study. Zero outside-human attempts have been recorded;
automated browser checks and AI critique do not substitute for those attempts.
No further recruitment or expert programme is required to try the labelled draft.

1. **State the question and strongest frontier case.** Explain how repeated
   AI-assisted research could become national leverage, name one condition it
   needs, and distinguish that possibility from Google’s bounded reported result.
2. **Change two premises and explain the difference.** Move diffusion earlier,
   then switch between existing deployment and process redesign. Explain what
   changed on the canvas and what did not change about ownership/household gains.
   Use the pinned before/after view; do not just report successful clicks.
3. **Find a source and classify a claim.** Open either national programme source
   and explain why its proposals are not realized outcomes. Then classify a 2030s
   branch statement as stipulation/conditional output, and explain why it is not
   an author forecast with an assigned probability.

Record the reader’s actual words, where they hesitate, and what they infer from
the arrows. Do not prefill success or manufacture quotations. Jinhua’s product
judgment, source reading, author/byline assent and publication decision remain
separate. The current draft may be tested without treating every future premise
as a blocker to prototyping.
