## What readers can now do

and international setting through roughly 2040. A strong compounding future is
fully developed before the reader changes conditions. Four controls alter
research feedback, diffusion ordering, physical process redesign and the
investment/political bargain. Four proposed futures and an interruption case
use the same executable qualitative rules. One canvas and a pinned comparison
show the consequences. No score, probability, empirical growth rate or computed
chronology is produced.

source access, original observations, project/capacity arithmetic, export and
browser coverage preserved. The finite routes are `/`, `/paper`, `/assumptions`,
`/work`, `/work/paper`, `/evidence`, `/methods`, `/about`, `/findings`. Archive
routes are retained. A versioned scenario construction and explicit scope/method
presentation amendment keep longer future chains separate from the accepted
eight coupling dispositions and historical external-scenario crosswalk.

supports one bounded AI-production improvement, not a recurring rate. DeepSeek’s

canonical sources nor approves any historical coding.

remain genuine publication blockers. They do not prevent testing this labelled

this work does not pretend the repository already has an open-source license.

It does not exhaust starting worlds, calculate feasible national budgets, model
