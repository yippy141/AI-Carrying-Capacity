# Source-verification and data-extraction pack for the first empirical figure in Frontier Is Not Fate

## Scope and coding rules

**Figure question being operationalized:** *How large is the gap between nominal AI adoption and deep, intensive, or workflow-integrated use?*

**Coding rule for comparability**

| Class | Rule used here |
|---|---|
| Directly comparable | Same source family, same survey universe, same period, same denominator, and same question frame; only the threshold or category changes. |
| Conceptually similar but not directly comparable | Measures point in the same substantive direction, but wording, denominator, universe, or period differs. |
| Not comparable | Measures different constructs altogether, such as firm adoption vs. self-reported worker uplift, or adoption vs. model capability. |

**Coding rule for official-claim status**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Official | Produced by a statistical authority or central bank as an official survey release, statistical article, methodology note, or census publication. |
| Official-adjacent | Produced by an official institution but as a staff blog, working paper, or summary built off official microdata. |
| Research | Produced by a research organization; useful context, but not official enterprise statistics. |

**Coding rule for independent-validation status**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| None | No separate independent source used to validate the exact claim. |
| Same-system triangulation | The claim is cross-checked against a questionnaire, methodology note, or same-institution working paper, but not independently replicated. |
| External triangulation | A distinct institution corroborates the same fact pattern. |

## Figure specification

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Figure type | **Multi-panel figure**, not a single pooled bar chart. |
| Core design principle | Show **within-source** nominal-to-deeper-use gaps only. Do **not** imply harmonization where none exists. |
| Panel set | **Panel A: ECB euro area intensity distribution.** **Panel B: Eurostat EU breadth thresholds.** **Panel C: U.S. BTOS current-use headline plus a separate inset/subpanel for breadth among adopters from the 2026 supplement.** **Panel D: Context strip for OECD and China, clearly marked as conceptual context, not pooled with Panels A–C.** |

| Panel C main | **BTOS headline bar**, x-axis: *U.S. employer businesses, AI in any business function*. y-axis: % of businesses. Plot the 2026-05-03 endpoint, 19.8. Include a note that BTOS current-use wording changed in late 2025. citeturn16view0turn16view2 |
