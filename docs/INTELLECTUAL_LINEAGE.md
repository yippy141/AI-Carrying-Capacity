# Intellectual Lineage

Status: M1.5 lineage register, updated 2026-08-28.

## Contribution boundary

Existing work measures jagged task capability, occupational exposure,
preparedness, adoption, diffusion, and scenario trajectories. Frontier Is Not
Fate extends this work by mapping sector-stage critical paths and
country-specific conversion frictions from accessible capability to intensive
integration and physical or institutional outcomes, with each link
evidence-graded and tested through deep sector modules.

The project does not claim to have discovered jagged capability, exposure,
preparedness, diffusion, technology adoption, weak-link production, or AI
scenario tracking. Public novelty language must name the prior line of work
before describing the extension.

## Lineage by function

| Function | Prior work used | What this project adds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jagged capability | Dell'Acqua et al.; METR time-horizon work | Maps capability differences onto sector-stage critical paths rather than inferring sector outcomes directly |
| Adoption and diffusion | Microsoft AI Economy Institute; Anthropic Economic Index; ECB, Eurostat, and Census surveys | Separates access, extensive adoption, intensive integration, and outcomes with source-specific denominators |
| National ecosystem and preparedness | Stanford Global AI Vibrancy; IMF AIPI; BIS exposure × readiness | Keeps ecosystem conditions as inputs or modifiers rather than a national conversion score |
| Task-chain economics | Chaining Tasks; O-Ring Automation; Jones on weak links; Jones on AI in R&D | Uses explicit stage DAGs and prevents averaging away binding stages |
| Firm conversion | BIS/EIB adoption-productivity work | Adds sector-stage mechanisms, evidence grading, and country modifiers; does not generalize one firm estimate globally |
| Diffusion and power | Ding; Ding and Dafoe | Tests diffusion and industrial-base mechanisms without presuming a US or China winner |
| Macro scenarios | OECD AI Meets Trade; World Bank MANAGE-AI; IMF AIPI | Treats modeled macro results as scenarios or preparedness inputs, not observations |
| Competing AI futures | AI 2027; AI 2040 Plan A; AI Futures Model; AI as Normal Technology; Citrini's 2028 crisis stress test | Audits cross-scenario premises—including possible economic-regime changes—instead of grading each scenario inside its own ontology |
| Data and figure practice | Epoch AI; Stanford HAI; METR; Our World in Data | Adopts provenance and restrained chart principles while preserving each source's license and not copying unique layouts |

## Verification rule

The canonical related-work register is
`data/literature/related_work.csv`. A row enters it only after its publisher,
DOI, or originating project page has been fetched and checked. Leads that
could not be verified, were underspecified, or named an unspecified tracker
remain in `data/literature/related_work_staging.csv`.

Verification confirms identity and access, not agreement with a work's claims.
It also does not grant permission to redistribute data. Dataset licensing is
tracked separately in `data/licenses/data_licenses.csv`.

## Attribution language

Use one of these labels where relevant:

- `concept adapted from`
- `data from`
- `original analysis by`

Do not copy third-party prose, code, unique layouts, or color systems. Do not
render a third-party dataset until its license row permits the intended use.
